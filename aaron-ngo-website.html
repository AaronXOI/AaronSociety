About Us

Born from a Passion
to Serve

Aaron Education Welfare Society was founded in 2009 by Oswin Terence in Saharanpur with a single belief — that every person, regardless of their background, deserves access to education, health, and dignity.

What began as a small community initiative has grown into a state-recognised, multi-purpose NGO touching thousands of lives each year. We work on the ground — in villages, schools, hospitals, and forests — alongside the communities we serve.

Our society is officially registered under the Societies Registration Act, Uttar Pradesh, and operates with full transparency and accountability.
